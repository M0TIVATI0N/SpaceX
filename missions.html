03 / HISTORY

Milestones in motion.

A timeline of experiments, firsts, and second chances that changed the economics of launch.

2002
THE BEGINNING

SpaceX is founded

Elon Musk establishes Space Exploration Technologies with the goal of making life multiplanetary.

2008
FIRST ORBIT

Falcon 1 reaches orbit

After three attempts, Falcon 1 becomes the first privately developed liquid-fuel rocket to reach orbit.

2010
NEW VEHICLE

Dragon returns safely

Dragon becomes the first commercial spacecraft to be recovered from orbit.

2015
THE LANDING

First orbital booster landing

Falcon 9 lands its first stage after delivering a payload, turning reusability into reality.

2020
CREWED FLIGHT

Dragon carries astronauts

Crew Dragon launches NASA astronauts, restoring an American crewed launch capability.

NOW
NEXT CHAPTER

Building a multiplanetary future

Starship development pushes toward a fully reusable transportation system for the Moon and Mars.
