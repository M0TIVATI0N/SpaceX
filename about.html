About Mission Hub.

A student-built guide designed to make a complicated space story feel close enough to explore.

WHY THIS TOPIC

Space is a design problem.

We chose SpaceX because its work connects engineering, visual storytelling, risk, and iteration. That gave us a subject with real data and a clear opportunity to create a memorable interface rather than a generic information page.

Mission Hub is an educational project, not an official SpaceX website. Our goal is to help a visitor understand the relationship between rockets, missions, and reusability in a few focused steps.

“The future is going to be exciting.”— a mindset behind every mission
